## Root Cause Analysis

### Issue 1: Trades DataFrame Schema Mismatch
The parallel sweep expects trades to have a 'pnl' column, but actual trades only have:
- `r_multiple` (R-multiple of trade)
- `price`, `quantity`, `commission`, `total_cost`
- No direct 'pnl' field

**Fix needed:** Calculate PnL from existing fields or update TradeAnalyzer to use r_multiple.

### Issue 2: Policy Logic Still Not Working
Even with simplified config, thresholds don't affect results:
- Sweep tested 576 configs (4x4x4x3x3 combinations)
- All configs failed before generating trades
- Suggests issue is earlier in pipeline (signal processing or order generation)

### Issue 3: Backtest Metrics Calculation
Sharpe ratio formula may be fundamentally wrong:
- Current: `(annualized_return - 0.02) / volatility`
- With intraday data: annualized_return calculation may be incorrect
- Volatility resampling may not be working as intended
